## Part 1 of 2

- Severity: major
  File: lib/bless_this_redmine_sso/hooks.rb:25
  Problem: The SSO login form action is hard-coded to "/oauth/sso/authorize" and ignores Rails’ relative_url_root. When Redmine runs under a sub-URI (e.g., /redmine), the form submits to a non-existent absolute path, breaking SSO.
  Failure scenario: Redmine is deployed at https://example.com/redmine. Visiting /redmine/login shows the SSO button. Clicking it submits to https://example.com/oauth/sso/authorize (404) instead of https://example.com/redmine/oauth/sso/authorize, making SSO unusable.
  Fix: Build the action URL with Rails route helpers so it respects relative_url_root. For example:
    - In the hook, compute the path once and interpolate it:
      path = Rails.application.routes.url_helpers.url_for(controller: 'oauth', action: 'authorize', only_path: true)
      and use <form action="#{path}" method="get">.
    - Alternatively, name the route in config/routes.rb (as: :oauth_sso_authorize) and use Rails.application.routes.url_helpers.oauth_sso_authorize_path.

- Severity: major
  File: lib/bless_this_redmine_sso/patches/account_controller_patch.rb:28
  Problem: The redirect URL is hard-coded to "/oauth/sso/authorize" in the login override and ignores relative_url_root, causing wrong redirects under sub-URI deployments.

  Resolution: as above, pre-existing, recorded, not fixed in the migration.
